The planner's Rolls table shows what every upcoming rare gacha pull gives you, from your seed. It reads like any Battle Cats seed tracker: two columns, track A and track B, numbered from your next pull downwards.

Where you are

Your next pull is cell 1A. Each pull moves you one row down the same track: 1A, 2A, 3A… The table shows every banner you've picked side by side, because all rare banners read the same seed — a pull on any of them moves you along the same rows.

Duplicate rerolls switch tracks

If a pull would give you the same Rare cat you got on the pull just before it, the game rerolls it to a different cat, and that reroll moves you to the other track. The table marks those cells and shows where the reroll lands you. This is how you change tracks on purpose: steer onto a duplicate and you jump across to the cats on the other side.

Guaranteed 11-draws

On a guaranteed event, an 11-draw gives the next 10 cells and then a bonus Uber. The table can show what that guaranteed Uber would be from any cell, so you can pick the row to start your 11-draw from.

Moving your seed along

  • The dice on a cell: "I rolled this". The table re-anchors so the cell after it is your next pull, and your saved seed moves with it.
  • ◁ back beside the seed steps back one roll; undo walks back through seed changes the app made for you.
  • Typing a seed by hand always works too.

Display options

Show cat names, icons or both, choose which form to show, how many rolls to list, and turn on Details to see each cell's rarity and slot seed if you want to cross-check by hand.
