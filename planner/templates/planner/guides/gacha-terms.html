The words this site uses, each in a sentence or two. Start here if any of it is new.

Seed
A number the game keeps for your account. It decides what every gacha roll gives, and moves on by one each time you roll. Know it, and you know every roll ahead of time.
Two seeds
You have two, and they have nothing to do with each other: one for the rare gacha (Rare Capsules), one for the Normal Capsules. Each has its own page here — the Planner and Normal Capsules.
Roll (or pull)
One draw from a gacha.
Single and 11-draw
A single is one roll (a Rare Ticket or 150 Cat Food). An 11-draw is eleven rolls at once for 1500 Cat Food.
Guaranteed
Some events promise an Uber with an 11-draw. That Uber is a bonus on top of the eleven normal rolls, so leave it out when you look up your seed.
Banner
One gacha event, like an Uberfest or a collab, with its own cats.
Track A and track B
The rolls ahead of you, shown as two side-by-side columns. You go down one of them, one row per roll.
Duplicate (reroll)
If a roll would give the same Rare cat you just got, the game rolls again and you jump to the other track. You can use this on purpose to reach cats on the other side.
Uber and Legend Rare
The two rarest kinds of cat. Uber is short for Uber Super Rare.
Plan
The order of rolls the Planner picks to get the cats you want for the fewest tickets and Cat Food.
