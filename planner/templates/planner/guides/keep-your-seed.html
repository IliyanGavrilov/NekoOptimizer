Your seeds are the one thing here you can't simply look up again: losing one means rolling and searching all over. Neko Optimizer keeps two, your rare gacha seed and your Normal Capsules seed, one pair per game version.

Where they're kept

Every change to a seed is saved to your profile on the server the moment you make it. The line under the seed field says Saved ✓ when it's done.

  • Without an account, your profile is tied to this browser by a cookie. Clear your cookies or switch device and it's gone.
  • With an account, your seeds, collection and plans follow you to any device. Signing up is free, needs no email, and keeps everything you've already done.

The ten-second backup

Even with an account, keep a copy somewhere of your own:

  1. Under the seed field, press copy a link to it.
  2. Paste it into your notes, or message it to yourself.

Opening that link later brings back the seed and the view you had, on any device. Writing the number itself down works just as well.

Opening someone else's link

A shared link shows its own seed, but it won't overwrite yours. The page says it's showing a shared seed and offers Make this my seed or Back to my seed. Nothing changes until you choose.

If a seed does go missing

Open your backup link, or find the seed again: the rare seed from a few rolls on any rare banner, the normal seed from a few plain Normal Capsules.
