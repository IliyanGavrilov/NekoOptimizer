Knowing your seed tells you what every pull gives. The planner goes one step further: tell it which cats you want and what you have to spend, and it finds the cheapest order of pulls across the banners that are running to get them.

1. Your seed

Enter it at the top, or find it if you don't know it. It's saved to your profile as you go.

2. Your targets

Pick the cats you want from the target list, grouped by the banners they're on. If you keep a wishlist in your collection, tick also search my wishlist to go after all of it at once. Cats you already own are left out of wishlist searches.

3. Banners and budget

The banners live today are picked for you; add or drop any. Then enter your rare tickets and catfood. One pull is 150 catfood, and the planner spends tickets first. If rare tickets are precious to you, raise their value in the advanced options and the planner will spend catfood to save them.

Not sure what you can afford? Turn on explore mode: the planner ignores your budget and finds the cheapest way to your targets within the rolls it looks ahead.

4. Find plan, then follow it

Press Find plan. You get the steps in order — which banner, single or 11-draw, and what each gives — walked along the Rolls table so you can see them. Do them in the game exactly as listed.

5. Apply it

Once you've rolled, press Apply on the plan. The cats it got are marked owned, your tickets and catfood go down by what it spent, and your seed moves to just after the last pull. The next plan starts from the right place.

Tips

  • Rare tickets are singles only — an 11-draw is always catfood.
  • If something you rolled doesn't match the plan, your seed is off. Re-find it rather than guessing.
  • Copy link shares your seed and Rolls view with a friend. Opening it doesn't change their own saved seed unless they choose to.
