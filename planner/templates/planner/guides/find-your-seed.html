Every rare gacha roll in The Battle Cats comes from one number, your seed. The game steps it forward with each pull, so once you know it, every roll you will ever make can be read off ahead of time. You can't see the seed in the game, but you can work it out from a handful of cats you have just rolled. That's what the Seed Finder does: it checks all 4.3 billion possible seeds, right in your browser, for the one that deals exactly your cats.

1. Roll a few times on one banner

Use any rare gacha event that's running — rare tickets and catfood work the same. Roll 7 to 10 times if you can (the minimum is 5) and note each cat you get, in order. Singles are easiest to record. An 11-draw works too: its results come in the order they were rolled.

Avoid events whose pool holds the same cat twice (some Busters events and collabs): the two copies look identical in the results, so the finder can't tell which slot you hit.

2. Leave the guaranteed Uber out

This is the mistake that breaks most searches. On a guaranteed event, an 11-draw gives 10 normal rolls plus a bonus Uber that doesn't come from the normal roll sequence. Enter only the 10 rolls before it, and nothing rolled after it. The same goes for the last roll of a step-up. The finder warns you when your 11th or 15th pull is an Uber, just in case.

3. Nothing in between

All rare banners share the one seed, so a roll on any other rare banner in the middle of your list breaks the chain. Record one unbroken run of pulls.

4. Enter them and search

  1. Open the Seed Finder and pick the exact banner you rolled on (search it by name).
  2. Fill in each pull, oldest first. Every box has a search: type a few letters of the cat.
  3. Press Find my seed. It takes a few seconds on most devices.

A quick check runs before the search. It flags the common slips — a likely guaranteed Uber, or the same Rare twice in a row (the game never deals that; it rerolls the repeat) — and lets you fix them or search anyway.

Several seeds match?

Your pulls fit more than one seed. Roll one or two more cats, add them to the end, and search again. Each extra pull cuts the candidates down fast, usually to one.

No seed matches?

Something in the list doesn't match what the game rolled. In rough order of how often it happens: a guaranteed Uber was included, the order is off, another banner was rolled in between, the wrong banner or run was picked, or a cat with a similar name was picked by mistake.

Once it's found

A single match is saved as your seed straight away, and the planner opens on it from then on. You only do this once: after that, the planner follows your seed as you roll. It's still worth a ten-second backup — see never lose your seed.
