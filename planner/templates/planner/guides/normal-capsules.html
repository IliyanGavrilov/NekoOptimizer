The Normal Cat Capsule doesn't use your rare gacha seed. It runs on a seed of its own, and every normal-side machine reads that same seed: the plain capsule, the Catfruit and Catseye event capsules and the Lucky Ticket machines. So one table on the Normal Capsules page predicts all of them, including which pulls land a Dark Catseye.

1. Find your normal seed

  1. Roll the plain Normal Capsules 8 to 12 times and note what you get, in order. They cost XP, so this is the cheap way to do it.
  2. Pick Normal Capsules, or Normal+ Capsules (w/ Superfeline) if Superfeline is in your pool. Once it joins it never leaves, and it changes every roll, so get this right first.
  3. Open Find my normal seed, enter the items oldest first, and search.
  4. Press the result's button to use it. The seed is then saved to your profile.

The pulls have to be one unbroken run: a roll on any normal-side machine in between breaks the chain. If nothing matches, check the capsule pick first, then the order.

2. Read the machines

Tick the machines you can roll right now. Each shows its own columns over the shared seed, with tracks A and B like the rare planner. The dice on a cell means "I rolled this": the table moves on and your seed is saved.

3. Plan a path

Open Plan a path, enter your Normal Cat Tickets and lucky tickets, and pick a target such as Dark Catseyes. The planner walks the seed with normal-ticket rolls and uses the odd lucky ticket to switch tracks, collecting as many as it can while spending as few lucky tickets as possible.

Your normal seed is kept separately from your rare one. Back both up: never lose your seed.
